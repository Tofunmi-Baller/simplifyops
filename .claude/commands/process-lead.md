Process a new lead for SimplifyOps. The user will provide at minimum a company name or contact name. Do the following:

1. **Research the lead thoroughly:**
   - Use WebSearch to find their company website, LinkedIn company page, and contact LinkedIn
   - Assess their current website: design quality, mobile responsiveness, speed, UX, content quality (rate 1-10)
   - Check LinkedIn for company size, industry, recent posts, hiring activity, growth signals
   - Check the contact's LinkedIn for role, tenure, activity
   - Identify specific pain points and gaps in their digital presence
   - Note what they actually need (website, app, automation, internal tools, etc.)
   - Check competitors' digital presence for comparison

2. **Grade the lead** using the criteria in `context/grading-criteria.md`:
   - Hot / Warm / Keep
   - Include a one-sentence reason for the grade

3. **Create a lead file** at `leads/[company-name-slug].md` with all research data structured as:
   ```
   # [Company Name]
   ## Contact
   ## Company Details
   ## Digital Presence Assessment
   ## Identified Needs
   ## Grade: [Hot/Warm/Keep] — [reason]
   ## Status: Researched
   ## Notes
   ```

4. **Update Notion** — search for the SimplifyOps Leads database and add or update the lead record with all data. If no Notion database exists yet, note this and continue.

5. **Report back** with a summary: company name, grade, what they need, and recommended next step.

Read `context/lead-process.md` and `context/grading-criteria.md` before starting.
