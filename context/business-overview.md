# SimplifyOps — Business Overview

## What We Are
A code and digital services agency. We build what clients need — fast.

## Core Process
1. **Lead intake** — receive leads, research them (LinkedIn, website, online presence)
2. **Lead enrichment** — gather all available info, fill gaps with research
3. **Lead grading** — hot leads, warm leads, keep/nurture leads
4. **Outreach** — personalized email that tells them we already built a prototype for them and asks if they want to see it. The cold email carries the booking link only; the prototype link goes out in the reply once they say yes
5. **Delivery** — build and ship within 2-3 weeks

## Philosophy
- We are service-flexible: we care about what the client needs, not a rigid menu
- If a client needs a website, we build it. If their real gap is something else (app, automation, dashboard), we flag it and handle it
- Speed is a core differentiator — what takes others months, we ship in weeks

## Services (flexible, not rigid)
- Website development
- Web app development
- Mobile apps
- Digital services (broad umbrella — automation, integrations, dashboards, etc.)
- Whatever the client actually needs

## Selling Points
- Speed: 2-3 week turnaround on work that typically takes months
- Quality: creative, professional output
- Tailored: every engagement starts with understanding the client's real need
- Prototype-first: leads get a working preview before they even sign

## Website Guidelines
- No faces or people shown
- Simple, clear language — no jargon
- Must be comprehensive enough that visitors don't need to look elsewhere
- Testimonials, video testimonials, portfolio
- Creative and visually impressive — not a generic template site
- Prove the value before the visitor even reaches out
