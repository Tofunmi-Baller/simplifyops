# SimplifyOps — Prototype Process

## What Is a Prototype
A working preview built specifically for a lead BEFORE they sign anything. It shows them exactly what we'd build, tailored to their business. This is our biggest differentiator — most agencies show generic portfolios. We show you YOUR thing.

## When to Build
- After a lead is graded Hot or Warm
- Before the outreach email is sent
- The prototype link goes in the email

## What to Build
Based on what the lead actually needs (identified during research):

### If they need a website:
- Build a landing page / homepage mockup as an artifact
- Use their actual brand colors, logo (if available), industry imagery
- Include their real business name and services
- Make it look like a finished product, not a wireframe

### If they need a web app:
- Build a dashboard or key screen mockup
- Show the core feature that solves their specific problem
- Use realistic data relevant to their industry

### If they need a mobile app:
- Build a mobile-responsive screen mockup
- Show the key user flow
- Use their branding if available

### If they need something else (automation, internal tool, etc.):
- Build a visual that explains what the solution looks like
- Could be a dashboard, a workflow diagram, or a simplified demo

## How to Build
1. Create as a Claude Artifact (HTML page)
2. Make it look polished — this IS the pitch
3. Use their brand colors if you can find them (check their website)
4. Include real content (their business name, services, industry terms)
5. Make it responsive
6. Save the artifact link — it goes in the outreach email

## Quality Bar
The prototype must be good enough that the lead thinks: "Wait, they already built this for us?" It should feel like we spent real time on it. This is what converts leads — not the email, not the pitch, the prototype.

## Storage
- Prototype artifacts are linked in the lead's Notion record
- The artifact URL goes in the outreach email
- Keep prototypes accessible — leads may share them internally
