# SimplifyOps — Lead Grading Criteria

## Hot (Priority — reach out immediately)
A lead is HOT when 3+ of these are true:
- Active business with revenue or funding
- Poor, outdated, or missing website/digital presence
- Recently hiring (growth signals — expanding team)
- Industry that values speed (startups, e-commerce, SaaS, agencies)
- Clear budget indicators (funded, established, charging premium prices)
- Has expressed a need or pain point (social posts, job listings mentioning "website redesign", etc.)
- Competitors have better digital presence (they're falling behind)
- Small-medium team (decision-maker is reachable, no enterprise procurement)

## Warm (Worth pursuing — reach out within the week)
A lead is WARM when 2+ of these are true:
- Decent but clearly improvable digital presence
- Some growth signals (new products, expanding)
- Probable budget but not confirmed
- Good industry fit
- Active online but not producing their own content well
- Could benefit from automation or internal tools

## Keep (Nurture — check back later)
A lead is KEEP when:
- Very early stage, likely no budget yet
- Already has a solid digital presence (they don't need us right now)
- No clear immediate need identified
- Large enterprise with long procurement cycles
- Good fit but wrong timing

## Grading Notes
- Always explain WHY a lead got its grade in one sentence
- Grade can change — a Keep that starts hiring becomes Warm or Hot
- When in doubt, grade higher (Warm over Keep) — better to reach out than miss
- Re-grade leads every 2 weeks if they haven't moved in the pipeline
