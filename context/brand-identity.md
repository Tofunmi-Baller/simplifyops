# SimplifyOps — Brand Identity

## Visual Direction
- **Theme**: Bold and dark
- **Backgrounds**: Dark (near-black, deep charcoal)
- **Contrast**: High — text and elements pop against dark backgrounds
- **Accent colors**: Neon or vibrant (electric blue, bright green, hot orange — TBD, to be refined)
- **Energy**: Modern, aggressive, confident — not corporate
- **Typography**: Clean, bold sans-serif — large headings, readable body text

## Content Rules
- **No faces**: never show people, photos of team, or headshots anywhere
- **No jargon**: every sentence should be understandable by someone who isn't technical
- **Concise**: say it in fewer words — cut anything that doesn't earn its space
- **Proof-driven**: show the work, show the speed, show the results — don't just claim it

## Identity
- Brand name: **SimplifyOps**
- No founder names or personal spotlights on any public material
- The brand is the identity — team-style references ("we", "our team") are fine
- No pricing displayed publicly — always "book a call" or "get a quote"

## Tone of Voice
- Confident but not arrogant
- Direct — get to the point
- Conversational but professional
- "We ship fast and it looks incredible" energy

## Visual Elements (for website and materials)
- Abstract shapes, geometric patterns, gradients — no stock photos of people
- Code snippets, UI mockups, device frames as visual elements
- Motion/animation where it adds energy (scroll effects, hover states)
- Dark-mode-first design
